# Three Kingdoms Tactics

An original 3D grid-tactics game for the browser, set in China's Three Kingdoms era. It is a fan-made spiritual successor to the *Dynasty Tactics* games. You lead officers on a war-council sand table, using facing and flanking, fire and wind, sworn-brother **linked stratagems**, and swaying enemy officers to your side.

**Unofficial, non-commercial fan project by evilgouki.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Koei Tecmo. The rules, names of systems, text, art and music are original. Historical people and events come from the public-domain *Romance of the Three Kingdoms*.

**Play:** https://dragoonant.github.io/three-kingdoms-tactics/ (Campaign: Liu Bei's Rise, 9 battles, or a Daxing Hill skirmish)
